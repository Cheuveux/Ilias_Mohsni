I'm Ilias, a videographer and editor based in P aris. I studied graphic design at ESAD Amiens (DNA 2023) and then moved to moving image.

I shoot and edit my own films: documentaries, music videos and brand films. This summer I filmed a campaign and an interview on Patmos, in Greece, with a Swedish runner from Dopest Runners Stockholm. Before that I made a 20-minute short and social videos for the French brand Le Petit Lunetier in Seville, four music videos for the artist Nass, and a documentary about Hammam Lif, my family's city in Tunisia,shot from 2022 and released in October 2025.

I'm also the communication manager of Koji.Japon, and a member of the collective ARC+, where I coordinate events and make video for our pop-up stores in Tokyo, Paris and Montreuil.

I'm open to documentary , brand and sport film projects.
